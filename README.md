<h1 align="center">Standing</h1>

A professional terminal for monitoring your social standing relative to the people you compare yourself to.

Standing maintains a Personal Standing Index across the Standard Classification of Comparison Dimensions, keeps a watchlist of Comparables, and records the level of standing at which you will feel secure.

It runs entirely offline. Nothing you record leaves your computer.
